Frequently asked questions

Do I need to schedule a call with you?

No. We do everything asynchronously by default. Send us your brief via email, we review it, and send you a proposal. If you'd prefer a phone conversation, just mention it — we can accommodate that.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

No. We handle the technical setup and explain everything in plain language. You just need to tell us about your business and what's painful.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Small to mid-size businesses (2–50 employees) in professional services, healthcare, marketing, e-commerce, and other industries where AI can cut costs or save time.

Why is private AI better than just using ChatGPT?

Three reasons: (1) Your data never leaves your network — important for client confidentiality. (2) No ongoing API costs — you pay once for setup. (3) You own the system — no vendor lock-in, no policy changes affecting your workflow.

How long does a project take?

An AI Waste Audit is typically delivered within 48 business hours from kickoff. Private AI setups take 1–2 weeks. Workflow automations take 3–10 days depending on complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What if it doesn't work out?

Every engagement has a defined scope and deliverables upfront. You won't be billed for work that doesn't meet what we agreed on. And our audits are low-risk — $500 fixed price to see exactly what you should do next.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We offer optional monthly retainers ($1,000–2,000/month) for ongoing AI work, maintenance, and new projects. But every initial engagement is a fixed-price project — no ongoing commitment required.
