Frequently asked questions

Do I need to schedule a call?

No. We do everything asynchronously by default. Send your brief via email, we review it, and send you a proposal. If you'd prefer to talk, mention it. We can accommodate that.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle the technical setup and explain everything in plain language. You just need to tell us about your business and what's painful.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Professional services, healthcare practices, marketing agencies, e-commerce, and any small team wasting money on tools that don't pay for themselves.

Why local AI instead of just using ChatGPT?

Three reasons: (1) Your data never leaves your network. Critical for confidentiality. (2) No ongoing API costs. You pay once for setup. (3) You own the system. No vendor lock-in, no policy changes breaking your workflow.

How long does a project take?

Spending Audits: 48 business hours. Local AI deployments: 1–2 weeks. Workflow automations: 3–10 days depending on complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What if it doesn't work out?

Every engagement has defined scope and deliverables upfront. You won't be billed for work that doesn't meet what we agreed on. If the system doesn't do what we agreed on, we fix it at no extra cost. Every project also includes a walkthrough and documentation. If your team can't use what we built within two weeks, we'll help until they can.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Optional monthly retainers ($1,000–2,000/month) for maintenance and new projects. But every initial engagement is fixed-price. No ongoing commitment required.
